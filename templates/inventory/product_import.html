File format

Use the first row for headings. Required columns are name, cost_price, and selling_price. Optional columns are sku, opening_stock, and reorder_level.

Prices use up to two decimal places; quantities use up to three. Enter numbers without currency symbols or thousands separators. A blank SKU is generated automatically. Supplied SKUs must be uppercase, use letters, numbers and dashes, exclude I and O, and contain no more than 20 characters. The preferred length is 8–12 characters.

Excel imports read the first worksheet. Replace formulas with values before upload. Files are limited to 5 MB and 2,000 product rows.
