1. Your legal rights come first

This policy describes LashPro’s voluntary change-of-mind returns process. It does not remove or reduce rights and remedies provided by Kenyan consumer law. Where goods are defective, unsafe, faulty, not as described or otherwise do not meet legal requirements, you may be entitled to a repair, replacement, refund or another remedy under applicable law. We will assess the circumstances and provide the remedy required by law.

2. Change-of-mind returns for products

In addition to your statutory rights, you may request a change-of-mind return within 7 calendar days after collecting the product, provided that:

  • the product is unused, unopened and in its original sealed packaging;
  • all accessories, labels and packaging supplied with it are included; and
  • you can provide the order reference or proof of purchase.

For hygiene and safety reasons, opened, unsealed or used lash, cosmetic and personal-care products are not eligible for a voluntary change-of-mind return. This exclusion does not apply to a valid complaint about a defective, unsafe, damaged-on-arrival, incorrect or misdescribed product, and does not remove any mandatory legal remedy.

3. Incorrect, damaged or defective products

Please inspect your items as soon as reasonably possible after collection. Contact support@lashpro.ke promptly if you receive the wrong item or believe an item is damaged or defective. Include your order reference, a description of the issue and clear photographs where helpful. Do not continue using a product that may be unsafe. We will review the complaint and arrange an appropriate lawful remedy, which may include repair, replacement, exchange or refund depending on the issue and the law.

4. How to request a return

Email support@lashpro.ke with the order reference, product name, reason for the request and your preferred contact details. Please wait for return instructions before bringing or sending an item back. Returns are coordinated through the studio; this website does not currently provide a postal return label or automatic online refund.

5. Refunds and exchanges

Where a refund is approved or required, it will normally be made using the original payment method where reasonably practicable, or another method agreed with you. Refund timing may depend on the payment provider or bank. A product order submitted online is not a payment: the storefront currently records an order request and payment is made at the studio. If an order is cancelled before any payment is collected, there is no payment to refund and the stock reservation will be released.

Exchanges are subject to stock availability. We will not charge you for an unavailable replacement without first discussing the options with you.

6. Appointments and services

Appointment requests are not confirmed until the studio confirms them. No online payment is taken when you request an appointment. You may cancel online at least 24 hours before the appointment start time; inside that window, contact the studio as soon as possible. If the studio cancels a confirmed appointment, it will offer a reasonable alternative date or, if you have already paid, arrange an appropriate refund. Concerns about a service should be reported promptly so the studio can investigate and discuss a suitable remedy. Statutory rights remain unaffected.

7. Contact

For a return, refund, exchange or service complaint, email support@lashpro.ke. Please include your order or appointment reference. We will acknowledge your request and explain the next steps.
