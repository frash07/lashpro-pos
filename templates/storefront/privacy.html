1. Who is responsible for your information?

This notice applies to the LashPro KE storefront, customer accounts, appointment booking and related studio services in Nairobi, Kenya (“LashPro”, “we”, “us”). LashPro is the business operating these services. For privacy questions or requests, contact support@lashpro.ke.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact and identity details: your name, email address and phone number when you place an order, create an account, request an appointment or contact us.
  • Account information: your sign-in email and a securely hashed password. We do not store your account password in readable form.
  • Order and appointment information: products, quantities, prices, order status, selected service, requested date and time, appointment status, and any notes you choose to provide.
  • Payment and transaction records: amounts, payment method, receipt details and, where you pay by M-Pesa at the studio, the transaction reference entered by staff. The online storefront does not take card or M-Pesa payments.
  • Technical and security information: session and security-cookie data, device/browser request information and logs needed to operate, protect and troubleshoot the service. The POS may also use browser storage for operational continuity and display preferences.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in optional notes unless it is genuinely needed for your request.

3. Why we use the information

We use personal information to:

  • create and manage customer accounts, order requests, stock reservations and collection arrangements;
  • receive, schedule, confirm, change and manage appointment requests;
  • contact you about an order or appointment, provide customer support and issue receipts;
  • record payments made at the studio, reconcile M-Pesa references and maintain business, tax and accounting records;
  • secure the service, prevent misuse, investigate errors and maintain reliable operations; and
  • meet legal obligations and establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We do not use the contact details collected for orders or bookings as permission for unrelated marketing. We will ask separately where consent is required for direct marketing.

4. Legal grounds

Depending on the activity, we process information because it is needed to take steps you request before entering a contract or to perform a contract with you; to meet a legal obligation; or for legitimate business interests such as security, fraud prevention, service administration and record keeping. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it. Withdrawal does not affect processing already lawfully carried out or processing that has another lawful basis.

5. Who may receive information

Access is limited to authorised LashPro personnel who need it to handle your request or run the studio. Information may also be processed by service providers that support the system, including:

  • Fly.io, for application hosting;
  • the configured database hosting provider, for storing customer, order and appointment records;
  • Cloudinary, where enabled, for product and service images;
  • the email delivery provider configured by the studio, when email delivery is enabled; and
  • technical content providers used by the staff POS interface, such as Google Fonts and the unpkg content delivery network.

We do not sell customer personal information. We may disclose information to professional advisers, regulators, law enforcement or other parties where the law requires or permits it, or where necessary to protect people, rights or the service. If you pay at the studio using M-Pesa, the payment provider processes that payment under its own terms and privacy notice; LashPro records the transaction information needed for reconciliation.

6. Storage, security and transfers

We use access controls, authenticated staff accounts, role-based permissions, secure connections where supported, password hashing, session protections, backups and operational logging to protect information. No internet service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please keep your sign-in credentials private and tell us promptly if you suspect unauthorised access.

Our technology providers may process or store information in Kenya or other countries. Where information is transferred outside Kenya, we will use a transfer ground and safeguards required by applicable Kenyan data-protection law. You can contact us for more information about the safeguards relevant to your information.

7. How long we keep information

We keep information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purposes described above. Account and contact information is retained while an account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards to deal with queries, disputes and security matters. Order, payment and accounting records are retained for the period required by applicable tax and other laws; tax records are generally retained for five years from the end of the relevant reporting period, subject to statutory exceptions or an ongoing proceeding. Appointment details and operational logs are deleted or anonymised when no longer needed for their operational, legal or security purpose.

8. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to:

  • tell you whether and how we use your personal information;
  • give you access to information we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate or misleading information;
  • delete false or misleading information, or request erasure where the law permits;
  • object to or request restriction of certain processing; and
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Some records must be retained to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes, so a deletion request may not require us to erase every record. To exercise a right, email support@lashpro.ke with enough information for us to identify your request. We may need to verify your identity before acting. You may also lodge a complaint with Kenya’s Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) at odpc.go.ke.

9. Cookies and similar storage

The service uses essential session, authentication and security cookies to keep your session working and protect forms. It may use browser storage for features such as remembering display preferences or preserving an in-progress POS transaction. These functions are operational rather than advertising features. Your browser settings can control cookies, but disabling essential cookies may prevent sign-in, checkout or booking from working.

10. Children

The storefront and appointment booking are intended for adults. A parent or legal guardian should contact the studio before submitting information for a person under 18. We do not knowingly request children’s personal information for independent account registration.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this notice when the service, our providers or the law changes. The current version and effective date will be posted on this page. For privacy questions or requests, contact support@lashpro.ke.
